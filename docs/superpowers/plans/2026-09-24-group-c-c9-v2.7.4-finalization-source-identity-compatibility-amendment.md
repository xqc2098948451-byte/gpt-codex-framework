identities. That requirement is incompatible with immutable WU003, which
requires seven source identities. For Finalization only, replace:



`acceptance.source_identities` list and `source_content_set_sha256` from
immutable WU003. Source-identity removal, reordering, substitution, or
recomputation from a reduced six-path set is forbidden.
